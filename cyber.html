Method

The register (cyber/cyber.db, SQLite in the model's repository) separates the event, the evidence for it, what it touched, its links to forecast questions, every model run, and every change delivered to a forecast. Unknown dates, actors and losses stay blank. Attribution is recorded with its basis (official, vendor, claimed, suspected). Evidence, model runs and forecast updates cannot be edited or deleted; events are never deleted, only marked retracted.

Feeds, read every three hours by the scheduled job: CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, FIRST EPSS scores for recent KEV entries, and NVD monthly publication counts. Ransomware and CVE-volume series entered by the analyst carry their source on this page.
